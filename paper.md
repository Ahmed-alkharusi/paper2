#Title
Two-neutron halos: A quantum tale of fragile stability

#Author
Jagjit Singh

#Introduction
The structure and reactions of exotic nuclei play a crucial role in astrophysical processes, particularly in nucleosynthesis. 
One such nucleus, 9C, and its associated capture reaction, 8B(p,γ)9C, have garnered significant interest due to their relevance 
in explosive stellar environments. Given the weakly bound nature of 9C, its structure is best described as a three-body system 
(7Be+p+p), though this has not been fully explored. A precise understanding of its continuum structure is essential for
 accurately modelling its reaction dynamics and extracting the astrophysical reaction rate for 8B(p,γ)9C. In 
this talk, I will present our recent three-body results of the ground and low-lying continuum states of 9C 
using the Gaussian-expansion method (GEM) combined with the complex-scaling method (CSM) [JS et al 2024]. 
Casal et al (2020) conducted study on 29F for N=20 in this IoI.
